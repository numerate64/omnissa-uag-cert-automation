Omnissa UAG certificate automation

This page is just a wrapper/instructions page. The real automation lives in renew-and-push-uag.ps1.

  1. Copy settings.example.json to settings.json
  2. Fill in secrets
  3. Run:
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\\renew-and-push-uag.ps1 -ConfigPath .\\settings.json
